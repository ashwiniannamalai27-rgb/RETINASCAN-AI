What is Diabetic Retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes-related eye condition that affects the blood vessels of the retina. Changes in these blood vessels can develop gradually, and early stages may not always produce noticeable symptoms.

Regular eye examinations and good diabetes management are important for protecting vision. RetinaScan is designed as an AI-assisted screening and educational support tool. It may help highlight patterns in retinal images and support the screening workflow, but it does not replace professional examination or medical diagnosis.

  • Screening support
  • AI-assisted review
  • Model prediction
  • Supportive tool for follow-up discussion
