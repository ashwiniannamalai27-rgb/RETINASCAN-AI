UNDERSTAND THE CONDITION

What is Diabetic Retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes-related eye condition that can damage the blood vessels of the retina. Changes may progress over time and can affect vision.

This is why regular eye screening and professional evaluation are important for people living with diabetes.

✓ Retinal changes can develop gradually
✓ Early stages may have few noticeable symptoms
✓ Regular screening supports better awareness
