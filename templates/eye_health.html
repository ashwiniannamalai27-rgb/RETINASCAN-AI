Eye health

Protect Your Eye Health

Manage Blood Sugar

Keeping blood glucose within the range recommended by your healthcare team can help reduce diabetes-related complications.

Monitor Blood Pressure

Healthy blood pressure management is important for overall vascular and eye health.

Stay Physically Active

Regular physical activity supports overall health and diabetes management.

Eat a Balanced Diet

Choose a balanced diet appropriate for your health needs and healthcare guidance.

Attend Regular Eye Examinations

People with diabetes should follow the eye-screening schedule recommended by their healthcare professional.

Take Breaks from Prolonged Screen Use

Regular breaks may help reduce digital eye strain and support eye comfort during extended screen time.

Healthy habits

Healthy Habits for Your Eyes & Overall Health

  • Regular physical activity
  • Taking breaks from prolonged screen use
  • Adequate sleep
  • Balanced nutrition
  • Diabetes management
  • Regular professional eye examinations

Eye exercises can be helpful for general comfort and digital eye strain, but they are not a treatment for diabetic retinopathy and should not be considered a preventive measure for this condition.
