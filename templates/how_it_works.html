Workflow

How RetinaScan Works

01

Upload Retinal Image

A retinal fundus image is uploaded to the platform and prepared for screening review.

02

Enter Basic Information

Basic patient information such as age, blood sugar, and blood pressure is entered for context.

03

AI-Assisted Analysis

The trained CNN analyzes the retinal image and estimates probabilities across five screening categories.

04

View Screening Result

The system presents the predicted category, probability information, visual explanation, and appropriate follow-up guidance.

Safety

Important Scope

RetinaScan is an AI-assisted screening workflow and educational tool. It does not replace professional examination, diagnosis, or treatment planning by a qualified healthcare professional.
